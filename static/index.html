LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS & TRANSPARENCY

Crop Supply vs Retailer Demand.

The core innovation of FasalSense: comparing available local supply against recorded retailer demand to detect market competition or potential buyer gaps.

ⓘ
Data Provenance & Policy:

FasalSense active listings and retailer inquiries provide dynamic platform proxy data. When platform counts are limited, synthetic simulation figures are provided for academic evaluation. Neither represents a census of the entire village. Pune APMC benchmark prices are historical wholesale references (MSAMB).
